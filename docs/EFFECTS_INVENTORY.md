# Combat effect sprite inventory

Source atlases (originals, never modified):

- `assets/effects/sources/combat_effects_atlas_01.png` (1672x941): near-black background, label above each sprite, 6x5 grid. **Atlas 01**.
- `assets/effects/sources/combat_effects_atlas_02.png` (1672x941): dark blue textured background, title bar, label below each sprite, 6x5 grid. **Atlas 02**.

The folder was delivered as `assets/effects/sources/` (plural); the brief said `source/`. The plural name is kept.

Sprites are generated by `python scripts/tools/extract_effects.py` (see its docstring for the method). Each is a transparent PNG cropped to its visible content plus 12 px of padding; titles, labels, divider ornaments and baked-in text were excluded by search boxes and masks. "glow" sprites encode brightness in alpha and are designed for additive blending on the dark combat screen (they look washed out on a light background); "solid" sprites (rock, smoke, vines) use matted alpha and normal blending.

Enemy actions reuse `wind/wind_slash` (attack) and `utility/barrier` (defend) as placeholders.

Total: **58 sprites** (32 reachable from card/status/potion events today).

## Not extracted (documented, not invented)

- **Inferno and Flame Wave from Atlas 02**: Inferno's flame tip crosses the atlas title divider and Flame Wave duplicates Atlas 01's cleaner version. Atlas 01 versions are used.
- **Atlas 02 title text, the "-2" number, all captions and Atlas 01 labels and divider ornaments**: text, not effects.
- **Dedicated Frost, Lightning, Wind and Earth *card* effects**: the atlases contain one sprite or two per element (Ice Shards; Wind Slash, Wind Gust; Chain Lightning; Earth Spike/Shatter/Stone). Those specializations are not playable yet, so their sprites are mapped by specialization rule only.
- No animation frames exist: each effect is a single still image animated by code (scale, fade, travel). A true frame sequence would need new artwork.
